# Citation verification notes

Checked 2 November 2026 against publisher or original-source records:

- **SHAP:** Scott M. Lundberg and Su-In Lee, “A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions,” *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 30* (NeurIPS 2017). The [official NeurIPS paper page](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/8a20a8621978632d76c43dfd28b67767-Abstract.html) confirms title, authors, and year.
- **CICIDS2017:** Iman Sharafaldin, Arash Habibi Lashkari, and Ali A. Ghorbani, “Toward Generating a New Intrusion Detection Dataset and Intrusion Traffic Characterization,” ICISSP, January 2018. The [official dataset page](https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html) identifies these authors and citation.
- **CSE-CIC-IDS2018:** The [official UNB/Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity page](https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2018.html) names the dataset resource “CSE-CIC-IDS2018 on AWS” and attributes it to a collaboration between CSE and CIC. The page does not give a publication year for the dataset page, so the manuscript cites it as n.d. and records the access date. The page lists the 2018 Sharafaldin et al. CICIDS2017 dataset paper as related methodology; it should not be described as the formal CSE-CIC-IDS2018 dataset paper.
- **UNSW-NB15:** Nour Moustafa and Jill Slay, “UNSW-NB15: a comprehensive data set for network intrusion detection systems (UNSW-NB15 network data set),” MilCIS 2015. The [UNSW Research dataset page](https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/unsw-nb15-dataset) confirms its title, authors, and year.
- **Common flow features:** Mohanad Sarhan, Siamak Layeghy, Nour Moustafa, and Marius Portmann, “NetFlow Datasets for Machine Learning-Based Network Intrusion Detection Systems,” first online 9 April 2021, pp. 117–135, Springer chapter DOI [10.1007/978-3-030-72802-1_9](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-72802-1_9). The [Springer chapter page](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-72802-1_9) confirms title, first/last authors, year, and page range; its full citation lists the middle authors as Layeghy and Moustafa.
- **Platt scaling:** John C. Platt, “Probabilistic Outputs for Support Vector Machines and Comparisons to Regularized Likelihood Methods,” in *Advances in Large Margin Classifiers*, MIT Press, 1999, pp. 61–74. The [National Diet Library catalogue](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/en/books/R100000136-I1572824500548904064) confirms author, title, publisher, and year; the cited chapter reference gives pages 61–74.
- **Isotonic/multiclass calibration:** Bianca Zadrozny and Charles Elkan, “Transforming Classifier Scores into Accurate Multiclass Probability Estimates,” *Proceedings of the Eighth ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining* (2002), pp. 694–699, DOI [10.1145/775047.775151](https://doi.org/10.1145/775047.775151). The ACM page was unavailable during checking; the bibliographic details were checked against the DOI record and author-hosted paper.
- **Calibration under dataset shift:** Yaniv Ovadia, Emily Fertig, Jie Ren, Sebastian Nowozin, Zachary Nado, D. Sculley, Balaji Lakshminarayanan, Joshua V. Dillon, and Jasper Snoek, “Can You Trust Your Model’s Uncertainty? Evaluating Predictive Uncertainty Under Dataset Shift,” *NeurIPS 2019*. [Official NeurIPS paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/9547-can-you-trust-your-models-uncertainty-evaluating-predictive-uncertainty-under-dataset-shift).
- **Brier score:** Glenn W. Brier, “Verification of Forecasts Expressed in Terms of Probability,” *Monthly Weather Review* 78(1) (1950), pp. 1–3, DOI [10.1175/1520-0493(1950)078<0001:VOFEIT>2.0.CO;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0493(1950)078%3C0001:VOFEIT%3E2.0.CO%3B2).
